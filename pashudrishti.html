AI-Powered Cattle Health & Breed Identification

Our advanced AI analyzes images of your cattle to instantly identify breeds, check for visual health indicators, and provide actionable insights. Built for the modern farmer.

Tips for Accurate Results

  • Use a clear, side-view photo of the animal.
  • Ensure good lighting, avoiding harsh shadows.
  • Make sure the animal is the main subject.
  • Avoid blurry or low-resolution images.
